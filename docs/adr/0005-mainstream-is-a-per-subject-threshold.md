# 0005 — Mainstream is a per-Subject threshold that holds

The v1 markers — Google Trends' daily top ten, the top-free app chart, top news — are dominated by sport, celebrities and news. The Subjects our discovery Sources surface (tools, products, technologies) almost never appear there, so Mainstream would never fire, Alarms would never close and lead time would never be measured.

We decided a Subject reaches Mainstream when one of its own measures crosses an absolute line and holds there: Wikimedia pageviews of its article at or above a weekly threshold for two consecutive weeks, or Google Ads average monthly searches at or above a monthly threshold in the last two months. Both thresholds are configuration, deliberately crude until Verdicts and the backtest tune them. A Subject that is already over the line when discovered goes straight to Mainstream and never alarms.

Consequence: Google Trends, the Apple chart and news feeds are no longer needed as markers and are deferred. Lead time is the number of days from the first Alarm to the Mainstream moment.
