# 0012 — App Store growth that holds and beats last year

The goal is a term the owner can build an app on: demand that keeps growing into next year, not a weekend's search. The first App Store week showed how far off it was. Of 40 terms proposed, the Classifier rated brands and events as the most specific (`ufc fight pass` 0.97, `game informer` 0.93, the marathons 0.82–0.88), and real needs the least (`ai maker` 0.56, `ai catch` 0.68), so needs went to Backlog while channels, news apps, four spellings of the White House app and five marathons filled the 20 places. Velocity made it worse: a term new to Apple's top 500 has no ranked baseline, so steady newcomers could never rise, while a one-week event jump could. Apple's history makes the difference visible: `chicago marathon` ranked 54, 66, 69 in October 2025 and again this October, `hurricane tracker` every late summer, `photos to pdf` flat around 55 all year, `pdf scanner` flat at 60.

We decided:

- **Discovery waits a week.** An App Store term is a Candidate only when it is ranked this week and last week, and climbed at least `min_climb` places since four weeks earlier (a newcomer from just below the list). A one-week jump is never proposed.
- **The Classifier is asked a different question for App Store terms**: is this a search for a kind of app or a task people want done ("pdf scanner", "marathon training plan"), rather than for one named brand, app, game, channel, publication, team, person or dated event ("ufc fight pass", "chicago marathon")? Its probability routes the term as the specific question does for the other Sources (ADR-0006). A term it rules out is Archived and takes no place, so at most `max_classified` climbers are asked a day to fill the `max_candidates` places. App Store Subjects proposed before this are asked once by the next daily run; only ones still in Watching or Backlog, never moved by a run and never seeded by the owner, are touched.
- **App Store search rises by Sustained growth, not Velocity.** Its series is measured 56 weeks back, which Apple keeps. A Subject grows when it is ranked this week and in at least three of the last four, and their median popularity is at least 3 points above the eight weeks before; a week below the list is ordered below every ranked week and never averaged as a number, so an even median takes the lower middle for now and the upper middle for before, and a week Apple no longer keeps is unknown, not below the list. If that median is also at least 3 above its best week in the nine weeks around the same week a year earlier, the growth is new; otherwise it is Seasonal.
- **New Sustained growth is an Alarm on its own**, and the Subject stays Trending while it holds. Seasonal growth only counts towards Corroboration. This supersedes ADR-0011's rule that an App Store-only rise stops at Rising: the one-week spike that rule guarded against can no longer count, and for app demand Hacker News and Stack Exchange are rarely the second Source.

The Classifier still never detects: it only says which kind of search a term is, and every rise comes from Apple's own numbers. The thresholds are a first guess, to tune against the owner's Verdicts. Apple's dataset still holds only each genre's top 500, so this catches needs entering the head of demand, never the long tail.
